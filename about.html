About the show

At your dealership, you are the fixit.

Running a powersports store is a hard business. Thin margins, big floor plans, seasonal swings, and a dozen departments that all have to work together. Dealership fiXit exists to make that job a little easier.

Every week we sit down with people who have figured something out: dealers who turn used inventory fast, managers who built sales teams that stick around, OEM and vendor leaders who see the whole market, and lenders who know which stores get approved. We ask the questions you would ask and we push for the specifics.

Who it is for

Owners, GMs, sales and internet managers, BDC teams, F&I, parts, service, and marketing. If you work in a powersports or motorcycle dealership, or you sell to one, you will find something here you can use.

How we think about it

  • Practical beats clever. We want you to leave with a thing to try, not a theory to admire.
  • Numbers matter. The used market, OEM stocks, and the Rundown all come with data you can point to in your Monday meeting.
  • Plain English. No jargon for its own sake. If a 5th grader could not follow it, we rewrite it.
  • Dealers first. Sponsors keep the lights on, but the show is built for the people on the floor.

The four shows

  • Operator Interviews (weekly): long-form conversations with dealers and industry leaders.
  • The Rundown (weekly): the week's news with the dealer angle, plus the market ticker.
  • NPA Market Update (monthly): Mike Murray from National Powersport Auctions on the used and wholesale market.
  • AI This Month (monthly): Brent Wees on what is real in AI for dealers right now.
