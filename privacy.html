Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Dealership fiXit ("we," "us") runs this website and the Dealership fiXit Podcast. This page explains what we collect, why, and what you can do about it. We keep it short on purpose.

What we collect

  • Information you give us. When you fill out a form (contact, guest pitch, sponsor request, newsletter), we collect what you type: usually your name, email, company, phone, and message.
  • Usage data. Like most sites, we may collect basic analytics such as pages viewed, device type, browser, and approximate location from your IP address. We use this to see what content is useful.
  • Embedded content. Episode players and market data load from third parties (YouTube, Spotify, Google). Those services may set cookies and collect data under their own policies.

How we use it

  • To reply to you and follow up on what you asked about
  • To send the weekly email if you signed up (you can unsubscribe any time)
  • To plan episodes, book guests, and work with sponsors
  • To understand what content people find useful

What we do not do

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share form submissions with sponsors unless you ask us to connect you.

Who else sees it

We use service providers to run the site and store submissions, such as our hosting provider (Vercel), database and CRM tools, and email tools. They process data only to provide those services to us.

Cookies

This site itself uses little to no cookies. Embedded players and analytics tools may set their own. You can block cookies in your browser settings; the site will still work.

Your choices

Email us at the address on the contact page to see, correct, or delete the information we hold about you, or to unsubscribe from anything. We will respond within 30 days.

Children

This site is for business professionals and is not directed at anyone under 18.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above. Material changes will be noted on this page.
